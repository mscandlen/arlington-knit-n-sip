Stitch in Space

Thursdays, 5–9pm · fibre space

fibre space is the yarn shop in the blue flatiron building in Old Town, and from 5pm on Thursdays they open the second-floor lounge to anyone who wants to bring a project and sit with other people making things. Knitters, crocheters and spinners, all levels, no booking.

Not on our line — take Blue or Yellow to King Street–Old Town, or the free King Street trolley down to Prince St.

Worth knowing

It's a shop rather than a bar, so no drinks — but the chairs are comfortable, the light is good, and it's the best fibre-arts room in the area. Worth the trip out to Old Town if you've never been.

Getting there

1319 Prince St, Alexandria VA 22314
King Street–Old Town (Blue / Yellow line) — about 10 min walk.
