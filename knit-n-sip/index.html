Knit'n'Sip

Arlington craft club & happy hour

A weekly drink with the knitting community, based in Arlington. Every Tuesday from 5 to 7pm we take over a corner of a bar, get something cold, and work on whatever's in our bags.

Knitting, crochet, embroidery, weaving, spinning, cross-stitch, felting, mending, punch needle — any fibre craft, any level, no experience required and nothing to sign up for. Every spot we pick is a walk from the Orange or Silver line, so you can come straight from work and get home easily.

the main event ~

Tuesdays, every week

Same time, same idea, rotating venue. We post the week's spot on Sunday, you turn up whenever you can between 5 and 7, and you leave whenever you like.

Some weeks there are four of us, some weeks fifteen. Nobody signs up for a season, nobody tracks attendance, and there's no project you're supposed to be working on.

Every date through October is on the calendar →

  • Look for the table with the yarn on it
  • Order whatever you want — or nothing
  • Stay ten minutes or the whole two hours
  • Chat the whole time, or sit quietly and stitch

and once a month ~

Two extras

Same crowd, different shape. Both are optional and both go on the calendar well in advance.

One Friday a month

A night in DC

We cross the river for a longer, later evening — cocktails instead of happy hour, and nobody has to be up early. Still on the same line: somewhere between Foggy Bottom and L'Enfant Plaza, never further.

One Saturday a month

A picnic in the park

Warm months only. Bring a blanket, bring a snack to share, bring your project. Usually Quincy Park by the Central Library, or Gateway Park up in Rosslyn — both a few minutes from the station.

completely free — no bar tab!
