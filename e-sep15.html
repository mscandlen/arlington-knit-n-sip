Ireland's Four Courts

5–7pm · Courthouse

An old Arlington pub that has never once hurried anyone. The main bar gets loud, so we take the side room, which is quiet, well lit and has tables you can actually spread out on.

Ask for the snug on the right as you come in. Guinness is the reason to be here.
