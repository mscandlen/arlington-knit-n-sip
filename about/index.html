the whole idea ~

About the club

Same time, same format, rotating venue. The week's spot is posted on Sunday. Drop in any time between 5 and 7pm and leave whenever you like.

Group size varies week to week. There's no sign-up, no attendance tracking, and no required project.

How a Tuesday works

  • Look for the table with a craft project on it
  • Bring your own project — you buy your own drink
  • Stay for ten minutes or the whole two hours
  • Chat, or work quietly — either is fine

A night in DC

A longer evening in DC, on the same line — between Foggy Bottom and L'Enfant Plaza.

Saturday picnic

A picnic in the park

Warm months only. Bring a blanket, a snack to share, and your project. Usually Quincy Park by the Central Library, or Gateway Park in Rosslyn — both a few minutes from the station.

Free — no bar tab

come along ~

Any skill level, any fibre craft. Bring your own project and buy your own drink.

Worth bringing

  • Any fibre project, or nothing at all
  • A metro card
  • Cash or card for your own drink
