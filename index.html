Knit'n'Sip

Arlington craft club & happy hour

A weekly drink with the knitting community, based in Arlington. Every Tuesday from 5 to 7pm we take over a corner of a bar, get something cold, and work on whatever's in our bags.

Knitting, crochet, embroidery, weaving, spinning, cross-stitch, felting, mending, punch needle — any fibre craft, any level, no experience required and nothing to sign up for. Every spot we pick is a walk from the Orange or Silver line, so you can come straight from work and get home easily.
