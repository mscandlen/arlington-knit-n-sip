Where we meet

We rotate through these stations. Everything listed is open on a Tuesday and has somewhere you can actually spread out a project.

  • Reston Station

    Founding Farmers · Lantern House

  • Tysons

    Barrel & Bushel · Founding Farmers

  • Dunn Loring

    Caboose Commons · Matchbox

    15 minutes' walk into Mosaic, or hop the free shuttle

  • Ballston

    Bronson Bierhall · Ballston Local · Quincy Hall

  • Virginia Square

    Quincy Park, for the Saturday picnics

  • Clarendon

    CIRCA · Kaldi's Social House · Brass Rabbit

  • Courthouse

    Courthaus Social · Ireland's Four Courts · Ragtime

  • Rosslyn

    Open Road · Continental Pool Lounge

  • Foggy Bottom

    CIRCA Foggy Bottom · Sixty Vines

    Friday nights

  • L'Enfant Plaza

    Easy Company, down at the Wharf
