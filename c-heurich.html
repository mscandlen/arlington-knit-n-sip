Stitch n' Sip Sundays

Sundays, 12–3pm · Heurich House Museum

A weekly afternoon gathering around the fire pits in the 1921 Biergarten, the beer garden in the old Heurich brewery mansion's castle garden. Knitting, crochet, weaving, cross stitch, felting — any fibre craft, any level, drop in.

Red line, not ours — Dupont Circle is an easy change at Metro Center.

Worth knowing

Local craft beer, cocktails, wine and non-alcoholic options, with a discount for stitchers. It runs weekly through to Thanksgiving. They ask you to RSVP so they know how many chairs to put out.

Getting there

1307 New Hampshire Ave NW, Washington DC 20036
Dupont Circle (Red line) — about 5 min walk.
