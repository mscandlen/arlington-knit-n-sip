Easy Company

5:30–8pm · L'Enfant Plaza

October's Friday in DC, and our furthest south. Easy Company is a bright, plant-filled room down at the Wharf, about twelve minutes' walk from L'Enfant Plaza along the water.

Walk down 7th St SW from the station. Worth leaving a little time — the walk is the nice part.
