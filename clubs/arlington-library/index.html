Knitting and Yarn Crafters

Monthly · Arlington Central Library

The library's own yarn circle: a monthly session in Central Library led by an experienced volunteer, open to knitting, crochet or any portable yarn project. All levels, free, and about as low-key as it gets.

The date moves around month to month — usually a weekend afternoon, 3–5pm. Check the library's events calendar for the next one, and RSVP there if you want a reminder email.

Worth knowing

Bring your own yarn and needles — the library doesn't provide supplies. Covered mugs of tea or coffee are welcome; this is the one on our list with no bar at all, which some weeks is exactly right. The library also runs yarn groups at its Cherrydale and Westover branches if Central's date doesn't suit.

Getting there

1015 N Quincy St, Arlington VA 22201
Virginia Square–GMU (Orange / Silver line) — about 4 min walk.
